1. Pros and Cons of agile

Pro: Dynamic and responds to new information quickly
Pro: Many more interaction is produced to make sure it aligns with objective
Con: Requires attention by all team members all the time (attention intensive)
Con: Timeline and budget changes throughout the project

2. Responsibities of Scrum Master and Project Owner

Scrum Master: Manages the team’s process and health
Product Owner: Define vision and goal about product

3. Types of Meeting

Daily Scrum: What was yesterday, what will be done today

Sprint Review: Show what’s completed, feedback from stakeholders and next steps

Sprint Retrospective: What went well or not, how to improve

4. Differntiate Epic, Story, Bug, and Task

Epic: Largest body of work
Story: feature and requirements
Bug: Problem
Task: Immediate to do
